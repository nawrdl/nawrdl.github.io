The North Australia Water Resources Digital Library welcomes contributions from individuals, organizations, and researchers. Any data or research relevant to the region's water resources and ecosystems is encouraged for submission, and all contributions will be thoughtfully considered for inclusion in the repository.

<section class="jcu-alert jcu-alert--important" aria-labelledby="contribute-heading" markdown="1">

## Contribute to the library
{: #contribute-heading .jcu-alert-title}

Please send your contributions to

<p style="font-size: 1.25rem; overflow-wrap: anywhere;"><strong><a href="mailto:tropwater@jcu.edu.au">tropwater@jcu.edu.au</a></strong></p>

Suggested subject: **NAWRDL contribution — [title or description]**

</section>

<section class="jcu-alert jcu-alert--note" aria-labelledby="request-heading" markdown="1">

## Request a document
{: #request-heading .jcu-alert-title}

To request a document, email [tropwater@jcu.edu.au](mailto:tropwater@jcu.edu.au) and include the **document ID** shown in the [library catalogue]({{ "/library/" | relative_url }}). Include the document title as well to help us identify it.

Suggested subject: **NAWRDL document request — [document ID]**

If you are requesting more than one document, include each document's ID and title in your email.

</section>

If you need assistance in accessing or downloading resources, please email us at the same address. Some publications are not publicly available and may require the permission of the author to access.
